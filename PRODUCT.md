# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Crunchie Wunche serves individual customers shopping for premium snacks, customers buying gifts and festive hampers, and businesses placing corporate or bulk gifting orders. They need to discover products quickly and narrow the catalog by occasion, dietary preference, and purchase context.

## Product Purpose

Crunchie Wunche presents curated snacks and gifting products as a premium, trustworthy ecommerce experience. The catalog should make product discovery effortless while supporting direct purchase, thoughtful gifting, and corporate-order exploration.

## Brand Personality

Premium, elegant, effortless. The voice is warm, confident, curated, and specific. The experience should feel editorial enough to communicate taste while remaining clear and conversion-focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketplace-like density, generic ecommerce dashboards, discount-first visual hierarchy, noisy promotional badges, and UI patterns that feel disconnected from the existing Crunchie Wunche homepage.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the established Crunchie Wunche visual language.
- Make discovery fast without making the catalog feel transactional or crowded.
- Treat gifting and corporate buying as first-class paths alongside everyday snacking.
- Keep every filter and catalog state shareable, reversible, and understandable.
- Make accessibility and performance part of the premium experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic HTML, useful ARIA labels, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, touch-friendly controls, and stable image dimensions to avoid layout shift.
